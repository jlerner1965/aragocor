Industries & applications

One mineral. Every room it walks into.

From row-crop calcium to glass batch to reef restoration — aragonite's purity, reactivity, and clean particle profile solve problems in markets that mined limestone can't reach. Nine of them, below.

ROW CROP · OMRI

Agriculture

OMRI-listed calcium for pH correction and nutrition. The orthorhombic crystal structure delivers faster availability than calcite, with fine particles that aerate soil and hold moisture.

≥ 98% CaCO₃ AG-CAL
MARINE

Aquarium & aquaculture

Gentle alkalinity and pH buffering for recirculating and pond systems, plus bioavailable calcium for shellfish, coral, and reef restoration.

pH 8.2 ± 0.1 AQ-CAL
ENERGY MATERIALS

Energy

High-purity CaCO₃ precursor for calcium-based intermediates and battery / energy-materials research, backed by a single-origin, traceable supply.

Single-origin EN-CAL
CEMENT & CONCRETE

Construction

Fine CaCO₃ filler for clinker substitution, ready-mix, and precast — supporting embodied-carbon reduction and workability in high-performance mixes.

1.42 g/cm³ CN-CAL
BATCH FEED

Glass

Low-iron, optical-grade CaCO₃ for container, flat, solar, and specialty glass. High whiteness and predictable batch chemistry, lot after lot.

≤ 0.04% Fe₂O₃ GL-CAL
REMEDIATION

Environmental

Buffering chemistry for acid mine drainage, calcium amendment for degraded soils and wetlands, and reef-friendly substrate for restoration.

< 2 ppm Pb EV-CAL
GCC FILLER

Ground calcium carbonate

Fine-ground and micronized oolitic GCC for plastics, coatings, and paper — high whiteness, low abrasion, and custom particle sizing on request.

D₅₀ to spec GCC
MUNICIPAL

Water treatment

Natural buffering toward neutral, phosphorus capture, and heavy-metal binding — a drop-in alternative to conventional limestone media.

pH 8.2 ± 0.1 WT-CAL
TURF & LANDSCAPE

Horticulture

Moisture-retentive infill for cooler surfaces, plus calcium for healthy root zones across golf greens, sports fields, and premium landscaping.

0.2–0.5 mm HT-CAL
