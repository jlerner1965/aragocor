Head-to-head

Aragonite vs. dolomitic lime.

Limestone is mined and ground. Aragonite is precipitated by living oceans and harvested gently. The difference shows up in dissolution rate, the pH ceiling, and the magnesium-to-calcium ratio your crop actually wants.

ARAGOCOR ARAGONITE DOLOMITIC LIME
Mineral form Aragonite (orthorhombic) Calcite + dolomite
CaCO₃ purity ≥ 98.4% 50–60% (Ca eq.)
pH ceiling 8.2 — soft buffer 9.0+ — hard spike
Mg load 0.6% — clean 20%+ — competes with K
Dissolution Demand-driven Slow, indiscriminate
Origin Ocean-precipitated Mined, kilned
Trace minerals Marine chelates Quarry overburden
