Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who we are

This site is operated by Aragocor Minerals LLC ("Aragocor," "we," "us"). We supply oolitic aragonite to industrial and agricultural customers. You can reach us at info@aragocorminerals.com or 209-487-0110, 2453 Grand Canal Blvd. Ste 200, Stockton, CA 95814.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us:

  • Contact / quote requests. When you use a form on this site, we collect your name and, if you provide them, your company, email address, phone number, area of interest, and the details of your message.
  • Correspondence. If you email or call us, we keep a record of that exchange so we can respond and follow up.
  • Basic technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider may log standard request data (such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited) for security and performance. We do not use this to identify you personally.

We do not knowingly collect information from children, and we do not ask for sensitive personal information.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your request for pricing, samples, documentation, or technical support.
  • To send information you have asked for and to follow up about your inquiry.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the website.
  • To meet legal, tax, and record-keeping obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

4. Service providers

Contact-form submissions are processed by our form provider (Formspree) and delivered to us by email. The site is served through our hosting provider. These providers process data on our behalf and only to deliver their service. We may also use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate traffic; if enabled, it is configured to avoid identifying individual visitors.

5. Cookies

This is a static marketing site and does not set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Any cookies used are limited to what is needed for the site or, if analytics is enabled, for aggregate measurement.

6. Retention

We keep inquiry and correspondence records for as long as needed to serve your request and to meet ordinary business and legal requirements, then delete or anonymize them.

7. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you have given us, or ask us to stop contacting you, by emailing info@aragocorminerals.com. Depending on where you live (for example, California residents under the CCPA), you may have additional rights; we will honor applicable requests at no charge.

8. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you send us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the current version.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email info@aragocorminerals.com or write to Aragocor Minerals LLC, 2453 Grand Canal Blvd. Ste 200, Stockton, CA 95814.
