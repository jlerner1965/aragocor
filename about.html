Our story

One deposit, done right.

Aragocor began with a simple observation: the same ocean-formed carbonate that builds Bahamian beaches is purer and more reactive than most of what the world digs out of the ground. Where others saw sand, we saw the cleanest agricultural calcium available.

We built a business around doing one material well — harvesting gently, verifying honestly, and publishing the numbers. No proprietary blends. No kiln. No asterisks. Just aragonite, graded and packed for the job in front of you.

Today that material moves by the bag and by the container load — into fields, water plants, glass furnaces, sports turf, and cultivation media across North America, Europe, and the Caribbean.
